SecuredStorage User Manual & Documentation

PQC-Compliant • Zero-Cloud • Hardware-Bound Security

🛡️ 1. Overview & Security Architecture

SecuredStorage is a hardened, zero-cloud, post-quantum cryptography (PQC) offline password vault designed for complete local sovereignty over your credentials.

Key Security Guarantees:
  • Zero Cloud Dependency: No telemetry, no background sync, no remote servers, and zero internet transmission.
  • Post-Quantum Cryptography: Hybrid ML-KEM-1024 (CRYSTALS-Kyber) lattice key encapsulation paired with AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption.
  • RAM-Only Residency: Decrypted secrets are kept in volatile memory only while unlocked and wiped to zero bytes immediately on lock.

🚀 2. First-Time Vault Initialization

When you launch SecuredStorage for the first time, you will be prompted to create your new encrypted vault:

  1. Master Password: Choose a strong, high-entropy password (minimum 8 characters, recommended 16+ characters).
  2. 6-Digit Hardware PIN: Choose a numeric PIN (e.g. 123456). This PIN works in tandem with Mac Touch ID or Windows Hello for instant two-step unlocking.
  3. Emergency Paper Recovery Key: Upon creating your vault, you will receive a unique 32-character recovery key (e.g. SEC-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-...).
⚠️ Absolute Zero-Knowledge Warning: Physically write down your Emergency Paper Recovery Key on paper and keep it in a secure lockbox or safe. Because SecuredStorage does not use cloud servers, there is NO "Forgot Password" email or remote reset. If you lose your Master Password, this paper key is the only way to recover your data.

🔑 3. Unlocking Your Vault (3 Authentication Paths)

SecuredStorage provides three distinct ways to unlock your database:

  1. Touch ID / Biometrics + 6-Digit PIN (Default Fast Unlock):
    Click Scan Touch ID and place your registered finger on your Mac Touch ID sensor (or Windows Hello scanner). Once biometrics pass, enter your 6-digit hardware PIN. This two-step local factor securely decrypts your vault key.
  2. Master Password:
    Switch to the Master Password tab to unlock directly using your full passphrase without needing biometric sensors.
  3. Emergency Paper Recovery:
    If you ever forget your Master Password, switch to the Emergency Recovery tab. Enter your 32-character paper recovery key, and you will be prompted to set a new Master Password and PIN to restore full access.

👁️ 4. Strict Privacy Mode & Ephemeral 10-Second Reveal

To protect your secrets from shoulder-surfing in public environments (cafes, offices, coworking spaces):

  • Default Masked View: When the vault unlocks, only the Site identifier is visible in plain text. Usernames, passwords, and sensitive notes are completely masked as ••••••••••••.
  • 10-Second Reveal (👁️): Click the Eye icon on any row to temporarily reveal that specific credential. An amber countdown badge displays the remaining time.
  • Automatic Re-Masking: After exactly 10 seconds, the row automatically re-masks itself. You can also click the Eye icon again anytime to manually re-mask immediately.

📋 5. Secure Clipboard Copying & Concealed Memory Protection

SecuredStorage includes native protections designed specifically to prevent credential leaks through the system clipboard:

Concealed Pasteboard (Anti-Cloud & Anti-History):
  • No iCloud Sync: Writes using native macOS concealed pasteboard markers (org.nspasteboard.ConcealedType). Apple iCloud Universal Clipboard is blocked from syncing your password to your other devices over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth.
  • Excluded from Clipboard Managers: Third-party clipboard history utilities (Alfred, Maccy, Raycast, Paste, CleanShot) are instructed not to capture or record your copied credentials.

Ephemeral Auto-Clearing Banner & Instant Flush:

When you click the copy icon (📋) next to any username or password, a banner appears at the bottom:

"Password copied to clipboard. Auto-clearing in 30s. [Clear Now]"

If you paste the password into your browser or login form within 3 seconds, you can click [Clear Now] to immediately flush the clipboard to empty bytes without waiting for the 30-second countdown to expire.

✏️ 6. Adding, Editing & Deleting Credentials

  • Adding Credentials: Click the + Add Credential button in the top header. Provide the Site identifier, Username, Password, and optional Notes. Click "Generate Strong" to generate a 20-character cryptographically secure password with mixed cases, numbers, and symbols.
  • Editing Credentials (✏️): Click the pencil icon on any row. For security, editing requires entering your Master Password to authenticate the action.
  • Deleting Credentials (🗑️): Click the trash icon on any row. Deleting permanently removes the record and requires entering your Master Password to confirm authorization.

💻 7. Moving Your Vault to Another MacBook

Because SecuredStorage is completely offline and file-based, you have 100% data portability. Your encrypted vault is stored in your operating system's standard application support directory:

~/Library/Application Support/SecuredStorage/vault.enc

  1. Locate Vault File: On your current Mac, open Finder, press Cmd + Shift + G, paste ~/Library/Application Support/SecuredStorage/, and copy vault.enc to an encrypted USB flash drive or AirDrop it.
  2. Install SecuredStorage: Install and launch SecuredStorage on your new MacBook (or create the directory ~/Library/Application Support/SecuredStorage/).
  3. Paste Vault File: Place your vault.enc file into ~/Library/Application Support/SecuredStorage/ on the new Mac.
  4. Launch & Unlock: Open SecuredStorage on the new Mac. It will automatically detect your existing encrypted vault and prompt you to unlock with your Master Password or Emergency Paper Recovery Key!

🛑 8. Anti-Surveillance Window Shielding

SecuredStorage activates hardware-level window capture protection directly with the macOS window server.

Screen Recording & Screenshot Blackout: Whenever screen sharing or recording applications (such as Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Slack huddles, OBS Studio, or QuickTime screen capture) attempt to capture the screen, the SecuredStorage window is rendered completely black in the capture stream, preventing accidental leakage during meetings or presentations.

💡 9. FAQ, Shortcuts & Session Inactivity

  • Auto-Lock on Inactivity: SecuredStorage automatically locks itself after 5 minutes of inactivity, immediately clearing all decrypted keys and memory.
  • Instant Lock: Click the red Lock Vault button in the header at any time to instantly lock.
  • Keyboard Shortcut: Press Esc at any time to close any open modal dialog or this Help guide.
  • Search: Use the search bar above the table to instantly filter credentials by Site name in real time.
Tip: Press Esc to return to your vault anytime.
